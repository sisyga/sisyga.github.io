About me

I came to mathematical biology through physics and a fascination with how simple interactions give rise to complex living systems. My work now connects evolutionary theory, cell-based simulation and experimental cancer research.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at TU Dresden’s Center for Interdisciplinary Digital Sciences. I earned my PhD in Mathematics there in 2024, following degrees in Physics.

I also enjoy making mathematical ideas accessible beyond research: I co-authored Mathematik der Pandemie, a popular-science book on modeling the pandemic.

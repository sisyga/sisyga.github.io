Morpheus.AI

An agentic workflow in which AI agents autonomously develop mechanistic models of cells and tissues in the simulator Morpheus: they turn a published study into a model, run it, inspect the output and revise it. The Morpheus skill is one of its components, and the benchmark tests the whole workflow on 15 studies.

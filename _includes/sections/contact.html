I welcome scientific exchange and collaborations on spatial evolution, cellular heterogeneity and treatment response, especially with imaging, cell-assay and single-cell data that can help shed light on tumor evolution and phenotypic plasticity.

Ongoing collaborations address cancer invasion and immunotherapy with the Friedl group (Radboud UMC, Nijmegen, The Netherlands) and glioblastoma heterogeneity with the Perez-García group (University of Castilla-La Mancha, Spain).

Students looking for a thesis project in mathematical biology are welcome to get in touch.
